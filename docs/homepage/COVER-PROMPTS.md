# Homepage-coverbeelden

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool. De gegenereerde PNG’s zijn voor de website als WebP geëxporteerd op 1536 en 768 pixels breed; geen tekst is in de afbeeldingen ingebakken. De diagrammen en formule zijn aparte HTML/SVG-elementen.

Bestanden: `assets/covers/modern/{rechtenwereld,wortelbouw,vectormissie,gravity-maze}{,-small}.webp`.

## Definitieve prompts

### rechtenwereld

A premium illustrated cover for a Dutch educational mathematics adventure game Rechtenwereld. Wide landscape illustration 1536x1024. Beautiful hand-painted lush green archipelago in turquoise sea, small wooden bridges between islands, warm sun, distant soft mountains, a tiny sailboat, clear blue sky. Main recognizable rocky green island at center-right, small lighthouse on distant left. Sophisticated storybook environment for teenagers, detailed but clean art direction, saturated turquoise balanced with sage green and warm limestone, contemporary premium educational game aesthetic. Composition must crop well to both wide desktop banner and 16:10 mobile cover; keep main island inside middle 60 percent. No characters, no interface, NO TEXT, NO NUMBERS, no formulas, NO GRAPH GRID, no logo, no watermark, no frame. Leave relatively calm sea/sky at left edge for website's separately rendered text area.

### wortelbouw

A premium illustrated cover for a Dutch educational mathematics garden game Wortelbouw. Wide landscape illustration 1536x1024. A refined charming small white rabbit with peach inner ears and a simple sage-green scarf sits beside several neatly arranged SQUARE grassy garden beds with light wooden edging, warm stone paths, a few golden flowers, subtle leafy plants, cozy garden wall in distance. Main rabbit slightly right of center, beds occupy lower central scene. Beautiful polished hand-painted storybook game environment for teenagers, not babyish, warm ivory sunshine, sage and rich leaf greens, sand and muted honey accents. Same premium digital painted quality as illustrated adventure game cover. Main rabbit and identifiable square beds stay within middle 60 percent so image crops beautifully as wide banner and 16:10 phone art. No UI, NO TEXT, NO letters, no numbers, no equations, no mathematical labels, no watermark, no frame. No fancy backpack. Soft quiet cream/green at left edge. The square garden beds evoke geometry; do not attempt a labeled Pythagorean diagram.

### vectormissie

A premium illustrated cover for a Dutch educational space mathematics game Vectormissie. Wide landscape illustration 1536x1024. Sleek compact silver spacecraft with glowing cyan engines flies diagonally toward upper-right across deep midnight navy space. Main ship center-right, atmospheric blue planet crescent low left, small scattered asteroids near lower corners, sparse tiny colored stars, a subtle distant indigo nebula. Sophisticated beautiful hand-painted concept game illustration, crisp spacecraft edges, cyan blue light with restrained magenta highlights. Not a warship, no weapon firing, not overly busy. Main craft and its readable silhouette inside middle 60 percent for both wide desktop banner and 16:10 smartphone art crop. Leave a little dark negative space on left and upper-right for web layout. NO TEXT, no numbers, no labels, NO ARROWS, NO GRID, no formulas, no UI, no logo, no watermark. Geometry and arrows will be real crisp SVG elements layered in the website separately.
