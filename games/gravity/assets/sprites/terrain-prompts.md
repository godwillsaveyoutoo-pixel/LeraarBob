# Generatieprompts voor muren en platforms

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde `image_gen`-tool. Bestanden: [wall-texture-v1.png](wall-texture-v1.png) en [platform-block-v1.png](platform-block-v1.png).

## Prompt voor de muurtextuur

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: seamless full-bleed material texture PNG for the FIXED walls, platforms and solid obstacle blocks in a 2D gravity puzzle game.
Primary request: one square, flat orthographic material swatch of dark sage-green / green-gray ceramic stone, matching the frame of an understated illustrated sci-fi portal. This is a texture map, NOT an isolated object or a photographed tile.
Scene/backdrop: the material completely fills the canvas, opaque from edge to edge with no padding, no transparency, no surrounding background.
Material: solid matte mineral ceramic with softly mottled olive-gray pigment, a small amount of fine worn grain, a few very restrained shallow wear marks. Broad quiet variations, gently lighter green-gray flecks and darker soft patches, limited contrast. Tactile and carefully painted, clean enough for a calm puzzle game for teenagers.
Color palette: dominant dark green-gray #46524d, variations close to #414d48 and #546159, subtle sage-gray wear around #647368. Stay dark and desaturated, with no white patches or black cracks. Match cream robot, tan counterweights, dark sage portal frames and cream background.
Composition: edge-to-edge uniform material coverage; seamless tileable on all four edges, evenly distributed detail and consistent average color. Straight-on flat orthographic view. Diffuse even illumination, no directional shading or highlights across the whole image. Square image.
Style: polished hand-painted 2D indie game material texture, soft broad strokes and restrained mineral grain. Detail must not compete with characters and arrows.
Constraints: no object outline, tile outline, panel borders, bevels, seams, bricks, individual stones, grout, cracks, bolts, screws, rivets, icons, lettering, numbers, arrows, symbols, perspective, shadow, vignette, lighting hotspot, scenery or extra objects. Output one opaque full-bleed PNG material texture.

## Prompt voor de vaste platformtegel

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one square structural BLOCK TILE sprite for the fixed platforms and obstacles in a 2D gravity puzzle game. It must look like stationary architecture.
Subject: a solid dark desaturated green-gray ceramic stone block viewed perfectly straight on. A broad flat square face with a restrained chamfer around all four edges, slim lighter sage bevels on top and left, dark bevels along right and bottom, gently worn edges, a few quiet shallow surface wear marks. Elegant solid machined-stone appearance. No box construction, no hardware. Mostly simple broad surface; details must read at 30 pixels square.
Style: polished hand-illustrated 2D indie puzzle game asset, tactile soft shading, matching a cream ceramic robot and sage-gray portal frames. The movable counterweight crates are warm tan with brown frames, so this fixed structural block must remain dark green-gray throughout.
Composition: FRONT orthographic elevation with square silhouette and straight right-angle corners. NO perspective, isometric view, visible top plane, visible side plane, protrusions or cast shadow. The block completely fills the square image from edge to edge, including the bevel. Opaque edge-to-edge, zero surrounding padding, zero background. A solid square ready to place directly beside another tile.
Palette: #46524d main face; subtly lighter #536159 mineral patches; muted sage #7c8f7c top/left chamfer highlights; #303c37 bottom/right edge. Dark and desaturated. Keep the inset face solid, never a hole or recess that could read as walkable.
Constraints: no text, arrows, icons, markings, bolts, rivets, screws, wood, border decorations, X braces, cracks crossing the whole block, loose rocks, broken corners, vines, moss, panels with separate pieces, glow or objects. One tile, not a spritesheet. Output an opaque square PNG.
